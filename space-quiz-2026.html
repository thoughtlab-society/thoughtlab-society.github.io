National Space Day · 24 August 2026

Space Quiz 2026

A celebration of astronomy, space science, teamwork, and curiosity at the Central University of Himachal Pradesh, Shahpur Campus.

24 August 20261:30 PM – 3:30 PMSeminar Hall · CUHP Shahpur
About the event

A campus celebration of space science

Thought Lab, a student-led initiative under Chandra STAR Club, in collaboration with the Physics Society, organised the Space Quiz Competition to celebrate National Space Day 2026.

Twelve teams from different academic programmes took part in an engaging competition designed to bring scientific knowledge, quick thinking, and teamwork together. The programme also included remarks from university leadership and a guest lecture, creating an opportunity for participants to explore astronomy and space science beyond the quiz itself.

Opening address

Remarks by Prof. Bhag Chand Chauhan

Prof. Bhag Chand Chauhan, Head of the Department and Campus Director, shared his remarks on university activities, the significance of Space Day, and the university’s Physics Society.

His address marked the opening of the programme and highlighted the role of academic and student-led activities in campus life.

Guest lecture

An added opportunity to explore astronomy

Alongside the competition, Chandra STAR Club organised a guest lecture by Prof. Hum Chand. The session gave participants an additional academic opportunity to engage with astronomy and space science.

The competition

Four rounds. Different ways to think.

The quiz combined theoretical, factual, visual, audio-based, and calculation-oriented questions. Buzzer-based rounds put participants’ speed, accuracy, and teamwork to the test.
