About Thought Lab

Thought Lab is a physics society built around curiosity, collaborative learning and engagement with scientific research. We believe learning science includes asking questions, examining evidence and explaining ideas honestly.

Our approach

Learn with care

Clear explanations, reliable citations, respect for original research, and honest discussion of uncertainty and limitations.

Contribute

Make something together

Students interested in writing, paper reading, projects or organising activities can contribute as the society grows.

Submission guidelines, team information and contact details will be published when ready.
