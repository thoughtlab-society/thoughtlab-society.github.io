A CRITICAL READ

Beyond the abstract.

A review should do more than repeat a summary. It should ask what a work sets out to explain, how it builds its case, what its limitations are, and who might find it useful.

Our reviews will distinguish the author's claims from the reviewer’s interpretation and link to the original source wherever possible.
